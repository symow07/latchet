## What the pattern says

Twelve names, ten unusable. That isn't bad luck — it's the category. Short, evocative English
words in personal finance are exhausted, and 2025–26 fintech launches are actively mining
classical antiquity (Stoa, Obol) and Latin/Romance roots (Lumo, Rivo, Solda) faster than a
shortlist can be screened.

Constraints worth carrying into any future round:

- **Avoid the cash / kash / kesh phonetic family entirely** — saturated in Finance on both stores.
- **Pre-check classical-antiquity nouns against 2025–26 fintech launches**, not just against
  established companies.
- **Screen the French homophone first.** It killed Poise (*la poisse*), Solda (*les soldes*),
  Obol (*obole*) and Kanto (*quanto*) — four of twelve, more than any other single test.
- Expect no name to come back CLEAR. The realistic target is *lightly* contested.

---

## Recommendation

**Take Fenn**, and drop the fennec rationale that generated it.

The fox story was the weakest part of the idea anyway — the fennec is **Algeria's** national animal
and *Les Fennecs* is the Algerian national team, so leaning on it in Maghreb marketing would have
misfired. Without it, Fenn is simply a clean four-letter name with the best cross-language profile
of anything screened:

- **Mandarin 分 (fēn)** is the 1/100 unit of the yuan and the verb "to divide" — a budgeting app
  named after the smallest unit of money and the act of splitting it. That is a better story than
  the fox.
- **Arabic فن = "art"** — positive, trivially pronounceable, no /p/.
- **French** — clean, no homophone trap, the double *n* blocks nasalisation.
- Icon: a division mark, a split, or 分 itself. Nothing zoological.

Live issues, both manageable: "Fen: Finance Tracker" sits one letter away in App Store search
(register the exact name early and lean on the doubled *n* in all creative), and `fenn.com` is
parked — budget for acquisition or ship on `fenn.app`.

### The alternative, which is not a lesser choice

The bundle ID `com.latchet.app` is **invisible to users**.
App Store *display* names can be changed in any later version submission. So:

> Buy `latchet.app` today → unblocks the Resend sending domain, the support email, and the <!-- TODO(domain) -->
> privacy-policy URL immediately. Ship v1 as **Latchet**. Rename the listing later if Fenn
> still appeals once the app is live.

Nothing except the icon genuinely requires a brand decision before submission. Four rounds of
screening have cost more calendar time than the naming is currently worth.
